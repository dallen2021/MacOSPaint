# Roadmap

## Foundation and vertical slice

- Multi-artboard model and freeform pasteboard.
- Sparse raster tiles, pressure-aware brush sampling, vector path records.
- Named undo transactions and atomic `.macospaint` persistence.
- PNG/PDF export seams.
- Selected native studio with light/dark appearance and pinned tools.

## Raster editing

- Brush, pencil, eraser, fill, gradients, clone, heal, blur, sharpen, smudge.
- Marquee, lasso, magic wand, feathering, transform, crop, and resize.
- Previewable, undoable destructive adjustments and filters.

## Vector and text

- Pen/direct selection, nodes and handles, primitives, arrows, editable text.
- Fills, gradients, strokes, snapping, alignment, grouping, and Boolean paths.
- Safe SVG import/export and vector-aware multi-page PDF export.

## Platform completion

- Vision-based subject selection and background removal.
- Tablet calibration, tilt, eraser, stabilization, and interruption recovery.
- Dockable/floating panels and saved workspaces.
- Accessibility, autosave/recovery, performance, and memory-pressure hardening.

## Later

- Signed and notarized releases.
- Automatic updates and possible App Store distribution.
- 16-bit/HDR/CMYK, PSD/AI/RAW, animation, plug-ins, scripting, and
  collaboration remain outside v1.
